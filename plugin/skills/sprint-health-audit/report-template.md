# Report template: sprint-health-audit

Use these sections in this order. Translate the headings into the user's language.

## Verdict

The sprint name, `healthScore`, and one sentence: on track, at risk, or off track, and why.

## Top risks

Up to 3. For each one:

- the check id;
- its value against the thresholds;
- what it means in one sentence;
- evidence: issue keys as links.

## Actions for the next 48 hours

2–4 concrete actions, each tied to a risk, with an owner role.

## Assumptions and limitations

Warnings (`NO_BASELINE`, `SMALL_SAMPLE`, unestimated work), cache age (`fetchedAt`), and anything that was not checked.
